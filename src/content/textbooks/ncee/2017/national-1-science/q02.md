**题目：** 若复数 $z$ 满足 $\dfrac{1}{z} = \dfrac{5}{2 + \mathrm{i}}$，则 $|z| =$

A. $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$

B. $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{5}}$

C. $\sqrt{5}$

D. $5$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 化简 $\dfrac{1}{z}$
> 📌 运用知识点：复数的运算

$$\frac{1}{z} = \frac{5}{2 + \mathrm{i}} = \frac{5(2 - \mathrm{i})}{(2 + \mathrm{i})(2 - \mathrm{i})} = \frac{5(2 - \mathrm{i})}{5} = 2 - \mathrm{i}$$

**第2步：** 求 $|z|$
> 📌 运用知识点：复数的模

$$|z| = \frac{1}{|2 - \mathrm{i}|} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4 + 1}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$$

**答案：A**
