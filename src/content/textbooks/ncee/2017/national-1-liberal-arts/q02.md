**题目：** 若 $(1 + \mathrm{i})z = 2\mathrm{i}$，则 $|z| = $（  ）

A. $1$

B. $\sqrt{2}$

C. $2$

D. $2\sqrt{2}$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 求解复数 $z$
> 📌 运用知识点：complex-number-operations

$$z = \frac{2\mathrm{i}}{1 + \mathrm{i}}$$

分子分母同乘共轭复数 $1 - \mathrm{i}$：

$$z = \frac{2\mathrm{i}(1 - \mathrm{i})}{(1 + \mathrm{i})(1 - \mathrm{i})} = \frac{2\mathrm{i} - 2\mathrm{i}^2}{1 - \mathrm{i}^2} = \frac{2\mathrm{i} + 2}{1 + 1} = \frac{2 + 2\mathrm{i}}{2} = 1 + \mathrm{i}$$

**第2步：** 求模长
> 📌 运用知识点：complex-number-operations

$$|z| = |1 + \mathrm{i}| = \sqrt{1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2}$$

**答案：B**
