**题目：** 若 $\mathrm{i}$ 为虚数单位，则 $\dfrac{5}{2 - \mathrm{i}} = $（  ）

A. $2 + \mathrm{i}$

B. $2 - \mathrm{i}$

C. $-2 + \mathrm{i}$

D. $-2 - \mathrm{i}$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 化简
> 📌 运用知识点：complex-number-operations

$$\frac{5}{2 - \mathrm{i}} = \frac{5(2 + \mathrm{i})}{(2 - \mathrm{i})(2 + \mathrm{i})} = \frac{5(2 + \mathrm{i})}{5} = 2 + \mathrm{i}$$

**答案：A**
