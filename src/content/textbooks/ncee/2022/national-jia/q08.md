**题目：** 已知 $f(x) = \log_3(x + 1)$，若 $f(a) = 2$，则 $a = $（  ）

A. $3$

B. $8$

C. $9$

D. $10$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 求解
> 📌 运用知识点：logarithmic-function

$\log_3(a + 1) = 2 \implies a + 1 = 9 \implies a = 8$。

**答案：B**
