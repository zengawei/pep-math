**题目：** 某射手每次射击击中目标的概率为 $p$（$0 < p < 1$），各次射击相互独立。设该射手在 $3$ 次射击中击中目标的次数为 $X$，若 $E(X) = 2$，则（  ）

A. $p = \dfrac{2}{3}$

B. $P(X = 1) = \dfrac{2}{9}$

C. $D(X) = \dfrac{2}{3}$

D. $P(X \geqslant 1) = \dfrac{26}{27}$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 确定参数
> 📌 运用知识点：binomial-distribution

$X \sim B(3, p)$。$E(X) = 3p = 2 \implies p = \dfrac{2}{3}$。A 正确。✅

**第2步：** 验证 B
> 📌 运用知识点：binomial-distribution

$P(X=1) = \mathrm{C}_3^1 \left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^1 \left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2 = 3 \times \dfrac{2}{3} \times \dfrac{1}{9} = \dfrac{2}{9}$。B 正确。✅

**第3步：** 验证 C 和 D
> 📌 运用知识点：expected-value-variance

$D(X) = 3 \times \dfrac{2}{3} \times \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{2}{3}$。C 正确。✅

$P(X \geqslant 1) = 1 - P(X=0) = 1 - \left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^3 = 1 - \dfrac{1}{27} = \dfrac{26}{27}$。D 正确。✅

**答案：A、B、C、D**
