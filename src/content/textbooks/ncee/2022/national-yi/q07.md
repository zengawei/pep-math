**题目：** 已知向量 $\vec{a} = (1, -1)$，$\vec{b} = (2, 1)$，则 $|2\vec{a} - \vec{b}| = $（  ）

A. $\sqrt{2}$

B. $\sqrt{5}$

C. $\sqrt{10}$

D. $5$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 计算
> 📌 运用知识点：vector-addition-subtraction

$2\vec{a} - \vec{b} = (2,-2) - (2,1) = (0,-3)$。$|(0,-3)| = 3$。

Hmm, 3 is not among options. Let me fix.

$\vec{a}=(1,-1)$, $\vec{b}=(1,2)$. $2\vec{a}-\vec{b} = (2,-2)-(1,2) = (1,-4)$. $|(1,-4)| = \sqrt{17}$. Not matching.

$\vec{a}=(2,1)$, $\vec{b}=(1,3)$. $2\vec{a}-\vec{b} = (4,2)-(1,3) = (3,-1)$. $|(3,-1)| = \sqrt{10}$. Answer C!

**题目：** 已知向量 $\vec{a} = (2, 1)$，$\vec{b} = (1, 3)$，则 $|2\vec{a} - \vec{b}| = $（  ）

A. $\sqrt{2}$

B. $\sqrt{5}$

C. $\sqrt{10}$

D. $5$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 计算
> 📌 运用知识点：vector-addition-subtraction

$2\vec{a} - \vec{b} = (4,2) - (1,3) = (3,-1)$。

$|2\vec{a} - \vec{b}| = \sqrt{9+1} = \sqrt{10}$。

**答案：C**
