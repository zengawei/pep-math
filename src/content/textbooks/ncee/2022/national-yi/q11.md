**题目：** 已知双曲线 $\dfrac{x^2}{9} - \dfrac{y^2}{16} = 1$ 的渐近线方程为（  ）

A. $y = \pm\dfrac{3}{4}x$

B. $y = \pm\dfrac{4}{3}x$

C. $y = \pm\dfrac{9}{16}x$

D. $y = \pm\dfrac{16}{9}x$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 求渐近线
> 📌 运用知识点：hyperbola-properties

$a = 3$，$b = 4$。渐近线 $y = \pm\dfrac{b}{a}x = \pm\dfrac{4}{3}x$。

**答案：B**
