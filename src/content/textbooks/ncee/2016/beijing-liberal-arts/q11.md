**题目：** 已知 $\sin \alpha = \dfrac{3}{5}$，$\alpha \in \left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right)$，则 $\cos 2\alpha =$ ______。

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 运用二倍角公式
> 📌 运用知识点：三角恒等变换

$$\cos 2\alpha = 1 - 2\sin^2\alpha = 1 - 2 \times \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2 = 1 - 2 \times \frac{9}{25} = 1 - \frac{18}{25} = \frac{7}{25}$$

**答案：** $\dfrac{7}{25}$
