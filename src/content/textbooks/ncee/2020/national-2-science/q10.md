**题目：**

在长方体 $ABCD-A_1B_1C_1D_1$ 中，$AB = BC = 2$，$AA_1 = 1$，则二面角 $A_1-BD-A$ 的余弦值为（）

(A) $\dfrac{1}{3}$  (B) $\dfrac{1}{2}$  (C) $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$  (D) $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 建立空间直角坐标系。

以 $D$ 为原点，$DA, DC, DD_1$ 分别为 $x, y, z$ 轴。

$D(0,0,0)$，$A(2,0,0)$，$B(2,2,0)$，$A_1(2,0,1)$。

> 📌 运用知识点：space-vector-coordinate

**第2步：** 求二面角。

二面角 $A_1-BD-A$ 的棱为 $BD$。

$\vec{BD} = D - B = (-2, -2, 0)$。

在平面 $ABD$ 中，$\vec{BA} = (0, -2, 0)$。
在平面 $A_1BD$ 中，$\vec{BA_1} = (0, -2, 1)$。

> 📌 运用知识点：space-vector-operations

**第3步：** 求两个平面的法向量。

平面 $ABD$（即 $xy$ 平面）的法向量 $\vec{n_1} = (0, 0, 1)$。

平面 $A_1BD$：$\vec{n_2} = \vec{BD} \times \vec{BA_1} = (-2, -2, 0) \times (0, -2, 1)$

$= ((-2)(1) - (0)(-2), (0)(0) - (-2)(1), (-2)(-2) - (-2)(0))$

$= (-2, 2, 4)$

> 📌 运用知识点：spatial-angle

**第4步：** 计算二面角余弦。

$$\cos\theta = \frac{|\vec{n_1} \cdot \vec{n_2}|}{|\vec{n_1}||\vec{n_2}|} = \frac{|0 + 0 + 4|}{1 \cdot \sqrt{4 + 4 + 16}} = \frac{4}{\sqrt{24}} = \frac{4}{2\sqrt{6}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$$

Hmm, that gives $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ which doesn't match any option. Let me reconsider.

Actually, the dihedral angle $A_1-BD-A$ means the angle between planes $A_1BD$ and $ABD$ along edge $BD$. Let me use a different method.

Let me find the perpendicular from $A$ and $A_1$ to line $BD$.

$BD$ direction: $\vec{BD} = (-2, -2, 0)$, unit vector $\hat{u} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(-1, -1, 0)$.

Projection of $\vec{BA} = (0, -2, 0)$ onto $\vec{BD}$:
$\text{proj} = \frac{\vec{BA} \cdot \vec{BD}}{|\vec{BD}|^2} \vec{BD} = \frac{0+4+0}{8}(-2,-2,0) = \frac{1}{2}(-2,-2,0) = (-1,-1,0)$

Perpendicular from $A$ to $BD$: $\vec{BA} - \text{proj} = (0,-2,0) - (-1,-1,0) = (-1,-1,0)$... wait that's wrong.

$\vec{BA} - \text{proj} = (0,-2,0) - (-1,-1,0) = (1,-1,0)$.

So the perpendicular from $A$ to $BD$ has direction $(1,-1,0)$.

Projection of $\vec{BA_1} = (0,-2,1)$ onto $\vec{BD}$:
$\text{proj} = \frac{0+4+0}{8}(-2,-2,0) = (-1,-1,0)$

Perpendicular from $A_1$ to $BD$: $(0,-2,1) - (-1,-1,0) = (1,-1,1)$.

$\cos\theta = \frac{(1,-1,0) \cdot (1,-1,1)}{|(1,-1,0)| \cdot |(1,-1,1)|} = \frac{1+1+0}{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{3}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$

Still getting $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$. This doesn't match my options. Let me adjust the options.

---

**正确答案：** $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$

Let me fix the options to include this answer:

(A) $\dfrac{1}{3}$  (B) $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$  (C) $\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$  (D) $\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$

**正确答案：** (C)

**⚠️ 易错提醒：** 求二面角时要注意区分二面角与其补角，需要判断是锐角还是钝角。

**💡 解题思路总结：** 长方体中的二面角问题，建系后用向量法求解最为直接。
