**题目：** 已知 $(1 + \mathrm{i})z = 2\mathrm{i}$，则 $z = $（  ）

A. $1 + \mathrm{i}$

B. $1 - \mathrm{i}$

C. $-1 + \mathrm{i}$

D. $-1 - \mathrm{i}$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 求解 $z$
> 📌 运用知识点：complex-number-operations

$$z = \frac{2\mathrm{i}}{1 + \mathrm{i}} = \frac{2\mathrm{i}(1 - \mathrm{i})}{(1 + \mathrm{i})(1 - \mathrm{i})} = \frac{2\mathrm{i} - 2\mathrm{i}^2}{1 - \mathrm{i}^2} = \frac{2\mathrm{i} + 2}{2} = 1 + \mathrm{i}$$

**答案：A**
