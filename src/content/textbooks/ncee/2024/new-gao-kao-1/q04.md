**题目：** 已知函数 $f(x) = x \ln x$，则 $f'(1) = $（  ）

A. $0$

B. $1$

C. $2$

D. $-1$

---

**解答：**

**第1步：** 求导数 $f'(x)$
> 📌 运用知识点：derivative-operations

$f(x) = x \ln x$，利用乘法求导法则 $(uv)' = u'v + uv'$：

$$f'(x) = (x)' \cdot \ln x + x \cdot (\ln x)' = 1 \cdot \ln x + x \cdot \frac{1}{x} = \ln x + 1$$

**第2步：** 代入 $x = 1$
> 📌 运用知识点：derivative-concept

$$f'(1) = \ln 1 + 1 = 0 + 1 = 1$$

**答案：B**
